# Fair control models v1.0.0

This separately versioned implementation detail document fills unspecified numerical choices in the unchanged `fly_open_loop_v1` protocol. It does not alter hypotheses, datasets, primary models, decoder amendment or search budgets. Definition and sources are locked before engineering verification. Source repairs during development are recorded; after a passing delivery, modified definitions require a new version/output location.

## Architectures and graph constraints

All rate matrices retain the exact 321 body IDs in frozen CSV order, unsigned normalized weights (counts/172), W[post,pre], and no self-loops. Blocks are directed presynaptic type×side to postsynaptic type×side; all 14 type/side groups, including single-neuron DN groups, remain distinct.

- Reference: exact frozen W_norm.
- Population average: total weight in each directed block divided over every eligible off-diagonal pair, including originally absent edges. Degree heterogeneity is deliberately removed; original sparsity/edge count is not retained.
- Primary weight null: 30 independent permutations of nonzero weights on the existing edges within each block. Topology, individual in/out degree, block totals and block weight multisets are preserved. This tests weight placement, not topology; no requirement to force a changed permutation or select a favorable seed.
- Secondary degree null: 30 independently seeded directed double-edge-swap chains within blocks. Reject equal endpoints, self-loops and duplicate edges. Retain degrees and block multisets; separately permute weights after swaps so neuron strengths are not implicitly constrained. Complete eligible blocks, blocks with one unique source/target, and blocks with <2 edges are structurally rigid and explicitly reported.

Null seeds are uint32 values from `SeedSequence([55001,10,kind,index])`, kind=1 for weights and 2 for degree rewire, index=0..29. Save all seeds before constructing matrices. Each mutable block targets 20 accepted swaps per original edge with at most 400 attempted proposals per edge. Save 20 progress checkpoints, acceptance, original-edge overlap and strengths. The operational mixing gate requires the complete target and observed topology turnover; failed chains remain saved and ineligible, never redrawn/excluded to rescue a result. This finite gate does not prove stationary independent uniform sampling; no such mixing claim is made. Rigid blocks are retained, labeled, and do not masquerade as randomized blocks.

Native condition keeps gain=.5. Secondary stability-matched condition uses one scalar gain = target_radius/rho(W), target=.6958315414466647, equivalent to rescaling W relative to .5. Zero radius retains fixed gain and is flagged. Save rho, continuous Jacobian eigenvalue real maximum and Euler spectral radius; require strict continuous/discrete stability. A stability failure is a failed architecture condition, never silently resampled. Clipping above 1% invalidates a linear-regime interpretation and stays visible.

## Matched information and timing

Every architecture receives the identical frozen 321-vector drive, including zero direct DN input, multiplied by .4 exactly once. No scenario identifiers, labels, geometry, future inputs or metadata enter forward passes. Encoder configuration/normalization are unchanged. Primary mapping is uniform population broadcast; the five synthetic mappings are separate conditions, common across architectures, never picked by outcome.

All models begin from zero state, without warmup; 1 ms steps for 1,000 ms; frames available only at their actual seconds timestamps using causal zero-order hold. Rate state x[k] and GRU state h[k] precede u[k]; input u[k] updates state[k+1]. Decisions use t=0..999 ms; endpoint state is diagnostic only. The direct feature-only control retains its earlier instantaneous causal convention. All models use the original closed [k-20,k] persistence and first-event latch; full-episode negatives and pre-onset alarms are retained.

Feature-only and two-coefficient nonnegative leaky-linear controls reuse Phase 8 equations and training-only fit. Population means are computed from the same vectors. For spatial inputs these shared within-population coefficients constitute an explicitly less expressive reference, not equal spatial capacity. They receive the full vectors before the predeclared reduction. Each spatial representation requires its own train/validation fit/calibration in Phase 11; no reuse of uniform thresholds as calibrated spatial models.

## Conventional GRU and fitting budget

One GRU, 321 input dimensions, 16 hidden units and scalar affine readout: 16,241 trainable parameters (three input/recurrent matrices, three biases, readout and bias). Score for the common nonnegative decoder is sigmoid(logit). Training BCE is computed stably on the maximum causal logit in [200,1000) ms, which is exactly BCE on the maximum sigmoid score. Ties use the earliest maximum. Pre-onset behavior is not optimized away or reset. No directional target/steering network is invented from the anatomical DN motor rule.

Seeded normal input/recurrent/readout initialization uses standard deviation 1/sqrt(fan-in); biases zero. Float64 exact BPTT over the entire episode, Adam beta1=.9, beta2=.999, epsilon=1e-8, global gradient norm limit 5, batch size 16, deterministic shuffled training order. Ten seeds 55100..55109, both LRs .0003 and .001, <=200 epochs, patience 20. Validation episode BCE selects earliest minimum checkpoint; select the smaller LR on an exact loss tie. Each LR starts from identical seed weights and training order. Validation supplies no gradient, normalization, class weights or optimizer update. Fixed clipping is an optimization safeguard and is counted, not a tuned hyperparameter. No class reweighting/dropout/weight decay.

The 16-unit size follows the protocol; it is not parameter-matched to the zero-trained-weight anatomical network. Trained references have more fitting opportunities, explicitly limiting interpretation. No additional recurrent gain normalization is applied to the nonlinear GRU gates; stability matching pertains to the rate matrices. Hidden states are bounded by the tanh/convex update. Record training/checkpoint counts, runtime, finite failures, input/output scales and parameter count. Failed trials are retained without seed replacement.

## Partitions, calibration and freeze

The training loader rejects test cohorts before file access, validates manifest identities, keeps families disjoint, and loads only frozen encodings. Its lazy records bound training memory to batches. Forward passes accept arrays only. Production fitting and calibration require exactly all 256 train and 128 validation records, with identities/labels/roles matching the saved manifest. Synthetic smoke has an explicit bypass of benchmark coverage solely for tests and can never create a test-authorizing freeze.

Each model, null seed, mapping and rate gain condition gets its own responses and threshold calibration: up to 31 equally spaced training-negative sustained-score quantiles advanced by nextafter toward +infinity, plus always-silent sentinel. Validation maximizes timely TPR subject to full-episode negative FPR<=.05, tie smaller FPR then higher threshold. Save all candidates and assessments and bind score hashes/IDs. Fitted model artifact hashes, complete sources/config, benchmark manifest and calibration payload are frozen before later held-out inference. Detect tampering; reject smoke-only/untrained checkpoints as scientific model freezes.

## Phase boundary and downstream rules

Phase 10 builds/validates all architecture artifacts and the full training/calibration machinery. Engineering tests use synthetic drive/movies and tiny synthetic training/validation samples, plus exact reference-only replay of one previously used training clip. No null-vs-circuit benchmark outcomes, benchmark GRU fitting, held-out neural/decision runs, bootstrap/statistical experiments, rankings, or new world evaluation are run. The job plan is not executed experiments. Ten saved initializations are explicitly untrained; smoke checkpoints remain synthetic-only.

Phase 11 must train all ten NN seeds at both rates for each mapping to be evaluated; fit the linear control on train; calibrate all architectures independently on train/validation; freeze all selected artifacts; only then evaluate untouched cohorts in matched pairs. H2 compares population-average and the mean of all 30 primary weight nulls. Any required failed native network blocks H2 confirmation; no survivor-only ensemble or best-shuffle selection. Separately labeled worst-case failure sensitivity (BA=0, FPR=1) is imputed, not measured and cannot confirm H2. Degree nulls and NN are secondary/descriptive, not substitutes for failed primary tests. Preserve family bootstrap, independent shuffle-seed resampling, 2,000 samples, fixed bootstrap seed, and predeclared intervals in the original protocol for later phases. World-domain calibration, adaptive steering, noise/encoder/biomechanics limitations remain unresolved.
