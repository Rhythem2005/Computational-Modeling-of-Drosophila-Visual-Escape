# Phase 9 short research review

Reviewed for this phase before implementation; sources inform choices, not biological calibration.

- [Card and Dickinson (2008), visually mediated escape planning](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18760606/): experiments motivate considering threat location and takeoff direction. Their findings do not validate our synthetic field assignment, DNp06 motor convention, movement speed or latency. These remain engineered assumptions.
- [Physically Based Rendering, 4th edition, sphere intersections](https://www.pbr-book.org/4ed/Shapes/Spheres): substituting a straight trajectory into a radius constraint gives a quadratic intersection problem. We apply this geometric idea in two dimensions to the relative motion of collision disks over each 1 ms world segment. Use closest-approach geometry and entry distance to avoid the most cancellation-prone quadratic-root form; count tangency and initial overlap. This is our environment implementation, not code copied from the book.
- [Gymnasium documentation, handling time limits](https://gymnasium.farama.org/tutorials/gymnasium_basics/handling_time_limits/): environmental terminal events and externally imposed time limits have different meanings. We record collision/boundary termination separately from horizon truncation. We do not install Gymnasium or implement reinforcement learning.

Inference/design decisions: a stabilized pinhole-style camera and front-facing circular billboards maintain compatibility with the frozen circle encoder while exposing changes in relative position and projected radius after action. The projection is an approximation, not spherical retinal geometry. Use the existing Phase 5 circle rasterizer and existing Phase 4 Euler step. The finite 1 s world horizon matches scheduling conventions but does not prove lasting safety. A transparent translation actuator is easier to audit than unsupported flight biomechanics; neither its fixed delay nor acceleration/speed/bearing constraints is a measured fly value.

World danger is an evaluator-only stationary-fly swept-path collision label. It cannot enter the encoder, neural simulator or decoder. Approaching objects can expand and still miss the fly; retain these fixtures and report any initiation as a false alarm under this new world definition. Closed-loop feedback correctness is an engineering gate, while efficacy and fair architecture comparisons require later experiment phases.
